Level: 1/5
🏃 Navigate through the algorithmically generated perfect maze!

🎯 How to Play

Use arrow keys or click direction buttons to move your character (🏃) through the maze and reach the flag (🏁).

Different levels have unique challenges: 🐺 Wolf Chase (escape the pursuing wolf!), 🏝️ Islands (unreachable areas break hand-on-wall), 🎯 Center Exit (exit in the middle!), 🚪 Multi-Exit (multiple ways out!).

These are perfect mazes generated with recursive backtracking - no dead ends or loops! The "hand-on-wall" algorithm works on most levels.

Tap direction buttons to navigate through challenging maze modes!
