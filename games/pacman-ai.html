Click anywhere or press SPACE to start! Yellow Pac-Man vs Red AI Pac-Man

How to Play:

  • Yellow Pac-Man (you) starts at the bottom
  • Red Pac-Man (AI opponent) starts at the top
  • Eat dots to score points (10 each)
  • Power pellets make ghosts edible for bonus points
  • Avoid ghosts unless you're powered up
  • The AI gets smarter as difficulty increases
  • First to eat all dots wins the round!
