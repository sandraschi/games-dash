👑 Royal Game of Ur

Ancient Mesopotamian board game dating back to 2600 BCE!

The Royal Game of Ur is one of the oldest known board games.

Race your pieces around the board using dice (or tetrahedrons).

Land on rosette squares for special bonuses!

Capture opponent pieces and reach the end first to win.
