武家時代

⚔️ The Samurai Era: 700 Years of Military Rule

For roughly 700 years (1185–1868), Japan was ruled not by Emperors, but by a military government known as the Shogunate (Bakufu). This period, known as the "Age of the Warrior" (Buke no Jidai), defined Japan's history, culture, and social structure.

🏹 Rise of the Warrior Class (Heian Period)

Late Heian Period (794–1185): The imperial court in Kyoto became obsessed with arts and poetry, neglecting the provinces. Local landowners hired private armies for protection. These armed guards became known as saburai ("those who serve").

Two powerful clans, the Taira and Minamoto, rose to prominence, eventually clashing in the massive Genpei War (1180–1185), which ended imperial rule forever.

⛺ Kamakura Period (1185–1333): The First Shogunate

Minamoto no Yoritomo established the first military government (Bakufu) in Kamakura, far from the Emperor in Kyoto.

Key Event: Mongol Invasions (1274 & 1281): The samurai, aided by a "divine wind" (Kamikaze) typhoon, repelled the massive Mongol fleets of Kublai Khan. However, the shogunate could not pay the warriors for their service (since there was no looted land to give), leading to its collapse.

🏯 Muromachi Period (1336–1573): Art & Chaos

The Ashikaga shogunate moved the capital back to Kyoto (Muromachi district). While political control was weak, leading to the Onin War and chaos, this era saw the birth of classic Japanese culture: Tea Ceremony, Noh Theater, and Zen Gardens flourished under samurai patronage.

🔥 Sengoku Period (1467–1603): Warring States

The "Age of the Country at War." Central authority collapsed completely. Local warlords (Daimyo) fought constantly for territory.

The Three Unifiers:
  • Oda Nobunaga: Looking to unify Japan through force and classic innovation (firearms).
  • Toyotomi Hideyoshi: Completed the unification but failed to establish a lasting dynasty.
  • Tokugawa Ieyasu: Won the Battle of Sekigahara (1600) and established the final shogunate.

🕊️ Edo Period (1603–1868): The Great Peace

Pax Tokugawa: 265 years of peace and isolation (Sakoku). The samurai transformed from warriors into bureaucrats and administrators.

Without wars to fight, martial skills became martial arts (Do), and the code of Bushido was formalized to give the idle warrior class a sense of purpose. Art and culture (Ukiyo-e, Kabuki, Haiku) exploded in the cities.

🌅 The End: Meiji Restoration (1868)

The arrival of Commodore Perry's "Black Ships" in 1853 exposed the Shogunate's weakness. Samurai from Satsuma and Choshu domains rebelled against the Shogun to restore power to the Emperor.

1868: The last Shogun abdicated. The samurai class abolished their own privileged status to build a modern nation, ending the Samurai Era.
