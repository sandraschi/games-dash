How to Play Arukone

  • Mouse Drag: Click and drag from one number to connect it with a smooth path
  • Connect Pairs: Each number appears exactly twice - connect them with continuous paths
  • No Crossings: Paths cannot cross each other or go through numbers
  • Fill Grid: Every cell must be part of a path (no empty spaces)
  • Straight Lines: Paths go horizontally or vertically, not diagonally
  • Right Click: Remove path segments
  • Validate: Check if your solution is complete and correct
