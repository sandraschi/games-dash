🚗 CAR PARK PUZZLE

Level: 1/15
🚗 Move cars to free the red car and escape!
Click a car to select it
Moves: 0

🎯 How to Play

The Objective: Maneuver the Red Car to the exit (🚪)!

🎮 Controls

  • Tap/Click a car to select it.
  • Use Arrow Keys or On-Screen Buttons to move.
  • Cars only move along their orientation (Horizontal or Vertical).

🧠 The Puzzle

This is a Block Shift Puzzle. You must move blockers recursively to clear a path.

Watch for Lorries (3+ cells) - they are harder to maneuver!

Use the Cortex AI solver if you need a hint!
