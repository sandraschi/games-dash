🎴 How to Play Zwanzigerrufen (2-player)

  • Goal: Win more than half the card points (45 of 88). Tarocks (I-XXI + Sküs) are permanent trumps.
  • Deck & deal: 40 cards — 20 tarocks (I, IV-XXI, Sküs) + 20 suit cards (K=5, Q=4, R=3, B=2, Glatze=0 points). All 40 are dealt: 20 cards each.
  • Bidding: Pass, Rufer (1 pt), Farbensolo (4), Solo (4). Solo beats Farbensolo beats Rufer. The Rufer calls the XX — in 2-player play the declarer plays alone.
  • Obligations: You must follow suit; if you cannot, you must play a tarock. You are never forced to beat (no Stichzwang).
  • Farbensolo: Tarocks do not trump suits; you may only play tarocks once your suit cards are gone.
  • Premiums: Trull (+1), 4 Könige (+2), Mond gefangen (+1), Pagat Ultimo (±1/±2), Absolut (±1/±2), Valat (+6). Solo doubles all premiums.
  • Declaring: The declarer may announce Absolut or Pagat Ultimo for double value — a declared premium that fails goes to the opponent.
  • Match: First to ±10 match points wins.
