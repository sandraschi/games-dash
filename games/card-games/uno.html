🎴 How to Play UNO

  • Goal: Be the first to empty your hand. Match the top card by color or number/symbol.
  • Deck: 108 cards - 4 colors (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue) with numbers 0-9, plus Skip, Reverse, Draw Two, Wild, and Wild Draw Four.
  • Skip (⊘): Next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse (⇄): Switches play direction (with 2 players, acts as Skip).
  • Draw Two (+2): Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn.
  • Wild (★): Play anytime and choose the next color.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4): Choose a color, next player draws 4 and loses their turn.
  • Drawing: If you cannot play, draw one card. If it is playable you may play it, otherwise pass.
  • Call UNO! When you are down to one card, press the UNO button. Forget it and you draw 2 penalty cards.
  • Scoring: Round winner earns points from opponents' hands (number cards = face value, action cards = 20, wilds = 50). First to 500 wins the match!
