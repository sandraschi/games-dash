📚 Seminal Papers Bibliography

Foundational papers that shaped modern AI, machine learning, and computer science

🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Attention Is All You Need
Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan N. Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, Illia Polosukhin
NeurIPS 2017
Introduced the Transformer architecture, revolutionizing natural language processing and forming the foundation for modern large language models like GPT and BERT.
arXiv PDF
ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks
Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever, Geoffrey E. Hinton
NeurIPS 2012
Introduced AlexNet, the breakthrough convolutional neural network that won ImageNet 2012 and sparked the deep learning revolution.
PDF Video
Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning
Volodymyr Mnih, Koray Kavukcuoglu, David Silver, Andrei A. Rusu, Joel Veness, Marc G. Bellemare, Alex Graves, Martin Riedmiller, Andreas K. Fidjeland, Georg Ostrovski, Stig Petersen, Charles Beattie, Amir Sadik, Ioannis Antonoglou, Helen King, Dharshan Kumaran, Daan Wierstra, Shane Legg, Demis Hassabis
Nature 2015
Introduced Deep Q-Networks (DQN), enabling AI to learn directly from pixels to play Atari games at superhuman levels.
Nature arXiv
Mastering the Game of Go with Deep Neural Networks and Tree Search
David Silver, Aja Huang, Chris J. Maddison, Arthur Guez, Laurent Sifre, George van den Driessche, Julian Schrittwieser, Ioannis Antonoglou, Veda Panneershelvam, Marc Lanctot, Sander Dieleman, Dominik Grewe, John Nham, Nal Kalchbrenner, Ilya Sutskever, Timothy Lillicrap, Madeleine Leach, Koray Kavukcuoglu, Thore Graepel, Demis Hassabis
Nature 2016
AlphaGo's victory over world champion Lee Sedol, combining deep neural networks with Monte Carlo tree search to achieve superhuman Go performance.
Nature AlphaGo Documentary

🎮 Game Theory & Reinforcement Learning

Multiagent Reinforcement Learning: Independent vs. Cooperative Agents
Michael L. Littman
SIGART Bulletin 1994
Seminal work establishing the foundations of multiagent reinforcement learning and the distinction between cooperative and competitive multiagent learning.
ACM
A Self-Play Policy Optimization Method for Zero-Sum Markov Games
Julien Perolat, Florian Strub, Vincent de Boer, Jerome Mary, Olivier Pietquin
ICML 2020 Workshop
Advances in self-play algorithms for competitive multiagent environments, enabling AI to learn complex strategies through self-competition.
arXiv

🧠 Cognitive Science & Human-AI Interaction

On the Measure of Intelligence
Shane Legg, Marcus Hutter
Artificial General Intelligence 2007
Proposed a formal definition of intelligence that has influenced AGI research and AI capability measurement.
arXiv
The Bitter Lesson
Rich Sutton
Incomplete Ideas 2019
Argues that general methods based on learning are ultimately the most effective and enduring approach to AI development.
Web
Superintelligence: Paths, Dangers, Strategies
Nick Bostrom
Book 2014
Comprehensive analysis of the potential development and implications of artificial superintelligence.
PDF

⚡ Software Engineering & DevOps

The Cathedral and the Bazaar
Eric S. Raymond
Book 1999
Influential essay on open source software development methodologies and the superiority of bazaar-style development over cathedral-style.
Web
Continuous Integration
Grady Booch, et al.
IEEE Software 1991
Early work on continuous integration practices that revolutionized software development workflows.
IEEE

🔬 Computer Vision & Multimodal AI

ImageNet: A Large-Scale Hierarchical Image Database
Jia Deng, Wei Dong, Richard Socher, Li-Jia Li, Kai Li, Fei-Fei Li
CVPR 2009
Created the ImageNet dataset that revolutionized computer vision research and enabled the deep learning breakthrough.
IEEE ImageNet
Very Deep Convolutional Networks for Large-Scale Image Recognition
Karen Simonyan, Andrew Zisserman
ICLR 2015
Introduced VGGNet architecture, establishing the importance of network depth in convolutional neural networks.
arXiv

This bibliography represents foundational works that shaped the modern AI landscape. Many of these papers are freely available online and remain highly influential in current research.
