📜 Historical Timeline - From Jomon to Modern Era
Japan's recorded history spans over 2,000 years of continuous civilization, with influences from China, Korea, and the West shaping its unique cultural evolution.
🗿 Ancient Era (Pre-300 CE)
14,000 BCE - Jomon Period 縄文時代: Hunter-gatherer society with rope-patterned pottery. World's oldest pottery tradition.

300 BCE - Yayoi Period 弥生時代: Rice agriculture introduced from China/Korea. Bronze and iron tools. First writing system.
🏛️ Classical Era (300-1185 CE)
710 CE - Nara Period 奈良時代: First permanent capital at Nara. Adoption of Chinese-style bureaucracy and Buddhism.

794-1185 - Heian Period 平安時代: Capital moved to Kyoto (Heian-kyo). Peak of classical Japanese culture. The Tale of Genji written.
⚔️ Medieval Era (1185-1603 CE)
1185-1333 - Kamakura Period 鎌倉時代: Samurai government established. Mongol invasions repelled (1274, 1281). Feudal system develops.

1336-1573 - Muromachi Period 室町時代: Ashikaga shogunate. Zen Buddhism flourishes. Trade with China resumes.

1467-1603 - Sengoku Period 戦国時代: "Warring States" - Constant civil war. Rise of Oda Nobunaga, Toyotomi Hideyoshi, Tokugawa Ieyasu.
🏯 Edo Period (1603-1868)
1603-1868 - Edo Period 江戸時代: Tokugawa shogunate. 265 years of peace. Cultural flourishing. Sakoku (isolation) policy.

1853-1868 - Bakumatsu Period 幕末: - The "Black Ships": Commodore Perry's American fleet forces Japan open after 220 years of isolation - Sonno Joi (Revere Emperor, Expel Barbarians): Samurai radicals demand restoration of imperial power - Sakamoto Ryoma: Revolutionary creates Japan's first modern navy. Assassinated in 1867 at age 31
🚀 Modern Era (1868-Present)
1868-1912 - Meiji Period 明治時代: Imperial power restored. Government-led modernization, imperial expansion, 15x GDP growth.

1912-1926 - Taisho Period 大正時代: Universal male suffrage, democracy movement, post-WWI prosperity, 1923 earthquake.

1926-1989 - Showa Period 昭和時代: Militarism, WWII, occupation, economic miracle (10% growth), bubble economy burst.

1989-Present - Heisei/Reiwa Periods: Lost decade, digital age, Tohoku disaster, Abenomics, technological leadership.
🔄 Patterns in Japanese History
Cycles of Change: Japan repeatedly imports foreign ideas (China, West) and adapts them uniquely.

Centralized Authority: Strong central governments alternate between imperial and military rule.

Isolation vs Opening: Periods of sakoku (closure) followed by rapid modernization when forced open.

Technological Leapfrogging: Japan masters imported technologies faster than the originators.

Resilience: Multiple disasters (earthquakes, wars) followed by remarkable recoveries.

Continuity: Despite constant change, core cultural values and social structures persist.
