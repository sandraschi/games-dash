🎯 Micro Chess Rules (4x4)

  • Board: 4x4 grid (files a-d, ranks 1-4)
  • Setup: King, Queen, Rook, Pawn per side
  • Goal: Checkmate opponent's King
  • Special: No knights or bishops - queens and rooks provide the power!
  • Time: Ultra-fast games, perfect for beginners!
