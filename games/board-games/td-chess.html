🖖 TRI-DIMENSIONAL CHESS

📜 Historical Database

Tri-Dimensional Chess is a multi-level chess variant that gained galactic prominence in the 23rd century. While it has several variants, the "Standard Rules" involve three 4x4 Neutral Boards and four 2x2 Attack Boards (White Queen's, White King's, Black Queen's, and Black King's).

> [!NOTE]
> The Tholian Web: Perhaps the most famous recorded match occurred between Captain James T. Kirk and First Officer Spock. The game is often used as a metaphor for Spock's superior logical capabilities, although Kirk's "human intuition" frequently allowed him to find unorthodox winning lines.

How to Play:

  • Vertical Movement: Pieces can move between boards if the path is logically clear in 3 dimensions.
  • Attack Boards: These $2 \times 2$ boards can be physically moved to different "pins" on the corners of the neutral boards if they contain no more than one piece.
  • Victory: Checkmate the opponent's King, regardless of which level he hides on.
