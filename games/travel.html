🎌 Travel Japan - The Budget Way
Japan is EXPENSIVE for tourists... except when it's not! The key is avoiding tourist traps and using local systems.

💰 THE YEN IS WEAK: Current rate ~150-160 JPY/USD. What costs $100 USD costs ¥15,000-16,000 JPY.

🏨 ACCOMMODATION - Skip Hiltons! - Guesthouses: ¥2,000-4,000/night (dorms ¥1,000-2,000). Clean, social, real Japan. - Business Hotels: ¥5,000-8,000/night. Tiny rooms but perfect for solo travelers. - Airbnb: Cheaper than hotels, especially outside Tokyo. - Net Cafes: ¥1,500-3,000/night for private booths (capsule-like but with computers).

🚆 TRANSPORT - JR Pass is KING: - JR Pass: ¥29,000-50,000 for 7-21 days unlimited JR trains. Pay for itself in 2-3 trips. - IC Cards: Suica/Pasmo in cities. Load ¥1,000-2,000, tap to ride. - Highway Buses: ¥2,000-5,000 for long distances (Tokyo-Kyoto 8 hours). - SKIP TAXIS: ¥5,000-10,000 for short rides. Use trains/subways instead.

🍜 FOOD - Supermarkets & Combini: - Convenience Stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart): Amazing prepared foods ¥300-800. - Supermarkets (AEON, Ito Yokado): Cook your own meals ¥1,000-2,000/day. - タイムセール (Time Sales): Half-price sushi and prepared meals! Check times - usually 7-8pm weekdays, 5-6pm weekends. Fresh sushi for ¥100-200! - Street Food: ¥500-1,000 for takoyaki, yakitori, ramen bowls. - Izakayas: ¥2,000-4,000 for small plates and drinks. - Conbini Breakfast: Onigiri + coffee = ¥300-500.

🎫 ATTRACTIONS - Smart Planning: - Temples/Shrines: Free or ¥300-500 donation. - National Parks: Free entry, beautiful nature. - Museums: Many free or ¥500-1,000. Tokyo National Museum free general admission. - Onsen: ¥500-1,000 for public baths, ¥2,000-4,000 for ryokan stays. - Festivals: Many free - cherry blossoms, autumn leaves, summer fireworks.
🗺️ Regional Highlights - Beyond Tokyo
Kyoto (京都): Ancient capital, 2,000+ temples. JR Pass essential. Stay in Gion district.

Hiroshima (広島): Peace Memorial Park (free), atomic bomb museum ¥200. Peaceful, thoughtful city.

Hokkaido (北海道): Nature paradise. Sapporo beer museum, lavender fields in summer, skiing in winter.

Kyushu (九州): Beppu onsen town, Kumamoto Castle, Nagasaki peace statue.

Nikko (日光): Toshogu Shrine, Lake Chuzenji. Beautiful autumn colors.

Kanazawa (金沢): Kenrokuen Garden (free), Higashi Chaya district geisha culture.
🎭 Cultural Experiences - Authentic Japan
Yosakoi Festivals: Summer dance festivals - energetic, joyful, free to watch.

Matsuri (Festivals): Local festivals throughout year. Food stalls, parades, fireworks.

Tea Ceremony: ¥2,000-5,000 for short sessions. Learn the ritual.

Calligraphy Classes: ¥1,000-3,000. Learn basic kanji writing.

Ikebana (Flower Arranging): ¥2,000-4,000 workshops.

Cooking Classes: Sushi making ¥3,000-5,000, ramen ¥2,000-4,000.
💡 Practical Tips - Avoid Tourist Traps
Transportation Apps: Hyperdia for trains, Google Maps for walking.

Language: Learn "arigatou gozaimasu" and numbers. Apps help with kanji.

Etiquette: Bow slightly, don't blow nose in public, remove shoes indoors.

Seasons: Cherry blossoms (April), autumn leaves (November) are crowded and expensive.

WiFi: Free in most stations, cafes. Prepaid SIM ¥2,000-3,000.

Health: Tap water safe, pharmacies everywhere (ask for "kusuri").

Safety: Extremely safe country. Petty crime rare.
💰 Realistic Budget - 2 Weeks
Budget Traveler: ¥80,000-120,000 ($530-800) - Flights: ¥60,000-80,000 round-trip from US - JR Pass: ¥29,000 (7 days) - Food: ¥20,000-30,000 (conbini + supermarkets) - Lodging: ¥20,000-30,000 (guesthouses)

Mid-Range: ¥150,000-250,000 ($1,000-1,650) - Business hotels: ¥40,000-60,000 - Some meals out: ¥50,000-80,000 - Attractions: ¥20,000-30,000

Luxury: ¥300,000+ ($2,000+) - Ryokans, fine dining, private tours
🌟 Why Japan is Worth It
Clean & Safe: Spotless streets, zero violent crime, efficient public transport.

Cultural Immersion: 2,000 years of continuous tradition meeting cutting-edge modernity.

Natural Beauty: Mount Fuji, autumn colors, cherry blossoms, hot springs.

Culinary Adventure: World's most diverse, high-quality food culture.

Technological Marvel: Bullet trains, robot hotels, ubiquitous automation.

People: Polite, helpful, culturally rich interactions.

Surprise Factor: Japan constantly surprises - nothing is exactly what you expect!
