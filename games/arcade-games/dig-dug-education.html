Dig Dug - Learn & History

The classic strategic digging arcade game

The Rules

Objective: Eliminate all enemies (Pookas and Fygars) on each level by inflating them with your air pump until they burst, or by crushing them under falling rocks.

How to Play

  • Use arrow keys to move and dig through the dirt
  • You can only move through dirt (you dig it) or empty tunnels
  • Press SPACE to fire your air pump in the direction you're facing
  • Hold SPACE to inflate enemies - they burst after 4 pumps
  • Avoid contact with enemies or you lose a life
  • Use fallen rocks to crush enemies for bonus points
  • Clear all enemies to advance to the next level

Scoring

  • Pooka (purple): 200 points when popped
  • Fygar (orange): 400 points when popped
  • Rock crush: 1000 points when a rock crushes an enemy (original)

Enemy Types

Pooka

Spherical purple creatures with goggles. They wander through tunnels and change direction when they hit walls. Inflate them 4 times to pop them for 200 points.

Fygar

Orange dragon-like creatures that can breathe fire. Inflate them 4 times to pop them for 400 points. In the original game, they could shoot fire through tunnels - be careful.

Strategy Tips

Basic Strategy

  • Create corridors: Dig tunnels to funnel enemies into predictable paths
  • Lure and trap: Lead enemies into dead ends, then inflate
  • Watch rocks: Rocks fall when the dirt beneath them is dug - use them to crush enemies
  • Don't trap yourself: Always leave an escape route

Advanced Techniques

  • Double inflate: Inflate one enemy while another approaches from behind - time the pop
  • Rock strategy: Dig strategically to drop rocks on multiple enemies
  • Corridor control: Create narrow passages so enemies can't flank you
  • Level awareness: More enemies spawn each level - plan your path

History

Dig Dug was developed and published by Namco in 1982. It was released in Japan on April 19, 1982, and in North America on May 1, 1982, where it was distributed by Atari.

Development

The game was designed by Masahisa Ikegami with help from Galaga creator Shigeru Yokoyama. It was programmed for the Namco Galaga arcade board by Shouichi Fukatani and Toshio Sakai, with music composed by Yuriko Keino in her first Namco game. Namco marketed it as a "strategic digging game."

Character

The protagonist is Dig Dug, whose real name is Taizo Hori. The game features 255 stages with increasing difficulty.

Cultural Impact

Dig Dug was the second highest-grossing arcade game of 1982 in Japan and achieved global success during the golden age of arcade games. It spawned sequels and spin-offs, including the Mr. Driller series, and has been included in many Namco compilations.
